v2.1.1 · self-hosted

Your saved recipes, finally ready to cook.

Recetario C imports the recipes you save on Instagram and turns them into recipe cards with scalable ingredients, video, a shopping list, a weekly planner and estimated prices. Self-hosted at home on your own mini PC.

What it does

Features

What already works in version 2.1, and what comes next.

  • Import from Instagram

    Step-by-step wizard: pick a collection, review the posts and choose video, photo or both. Detects duplicates.

    Available

  • Videos and cooking mode

    The original video is stored on your server. Cooking mode with large steps and timers.

    Available

  • Personalised shopping list

    Adds up ingredients from recipes and the plan, merges units and skips what is already in your pantry.

    Available

  • Pantry

    Staples and recurring items kept off the list. Stock quantities and deduction when you cook: in phase 3.

    Basic · stock coming soon

  • Weekly planner

    Plan lunches and dinners; servings start from each recipe and flow into the list.

    Available

  • Estimated prices

    Cost per serving and total for every recipe, always labelled as an estimate.

    Available

  • Statistics and “cheaper now”

    Price history, most-cooked recipes and alerts when a recipe is cheaper than its average.

    Coming soon

  • AI and MCP

    “Connect your AI” with OpenAI, OpenRouter or local Ollama (encrypted keys). REST API and MCP server with read or write tokens.

    Available

Tech stack

No frontend frameworks and no mandatory third-party services.

  • FastAPI + UvicornPython API and web server
  • SQLiteSingle-file database with migrations
  • Vanilla JavaScriptSingle-page interface, no build step
  • MCPModel Context Protocol server (HTTP and stdio)
  • InstaloaderReads saved collections
  • ffmpegVideo covers and thumbnails
  • cryptography + Argon2Fernet-encrypted AI keys, Argon2 password hashes
  • pytest + PlaywrightAutomated tests and screenshots
